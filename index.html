selected publications

  1. Working Paper
    Adaptive Adjustment of Higher Education Majors Under LLM Shocks: Theoretical Modeling, Exposure Measurement, and Continuous DID Estimation
    Mengqi Wei, Xiaodong Chen, and Xiaoliang Yang
    Working Paper, Jinhe Center for Economic Research, Xi’an Jiaotong University, 2026
    Abs Bib

    Extends the Acemoglu & Restrepo (2018) task framework to higher education supply. Constructs an occupational LLM exposure crosswalk for 889 undergraduate majors and evaluates enrollment adjustments with a 2015–2024 micro panel of 4.34 million provincial admission records using continuous difference-in-differences and causal forests.

  2. Project
    Claim2Value: Financial AI Agent for Technical Claim Verification and Dynamic Valuation
    Mengqi Wei and Team
    PKU Financial AI Agent Competition (Awarded Project), 2026
    Abs Bib Code

    An AI agent pipeline designed for embodied AI and humanoid robotics supply chains. Extracts technical claims from disclosures, verifies specifications against industry benchmarks, and maps technical metrics into multi-scenario financial valuation models.
